# Quarterly Close Procedure (Finance only)

## Timeline
The quarterly financial close must be completed within 7 business days after quarter end. Preliminary results are shared with the CFO on business day 5.

## Accruals
Any unbilled expense above $10,000 must be accrued in the quarter in which the service was received.

## Journal entries
Manual journal entries above $50,000 require approval from the Financial Controller before posting. All journal entries need supporting documentation attached in the ERP.

## Reconciliations
Bank and intercompany reconciliations must be completed and reviewed by business day 4.
