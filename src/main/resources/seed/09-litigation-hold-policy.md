# Litigation Hold Policy (Legal only)

## When a hold is issued
The Legal team issues a litigation hold notice when litigation, a regulatory investigation or an audit is reasonably anticipated.

## Obligations
Custodians who receive a hold notice must preserve all relevant emails, documents and chat messages, and must suspend any automatic deletion. Custodians must acknowledge the hold notice within 2 business days.

## Release
Only the General Counsel can release a litigation hold, and custodians are notified in writing when it is released.
