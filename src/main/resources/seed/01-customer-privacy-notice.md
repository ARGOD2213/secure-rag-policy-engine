# Customer Privacy Notice

Northwind Labs is a fictional company used for this demo. This notice explains how Northwind Labs handles personal data belonging to customers.

## Data we collect
We collect account details (name, work email, company), billing information, and product usage telemetry. We do not collect sensitive categories of data such as health or biometric information.

## Retention
Customer account data is retained for 24 months after an account is closed, after which it is permanently deleted. Billing records are kept for 7 years to meet tax obligations.

## Your rights
Customers may request a copy of their data, a correction, or deletion by emailing privacy@northwind.example. We respond to every data subject request within 30 days.

## Sharing
Northwind Labs never sells customer data. Data is shared only with sub-processors listed on our website, all of whom are bound by data processing agreements.
