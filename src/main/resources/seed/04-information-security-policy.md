# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be stored in the company password manager. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, source control and every production system.

## Devices
All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Customer data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage.

## Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents, including lost devices and phishing emails that were clicked, must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## Access reviews
Managers review their team's system access every quarter and remove access that is no longer needed.
