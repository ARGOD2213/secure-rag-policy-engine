# Compensation Bands and Salary Review (HR confidential)

## Engineering salary bands (base salary, USD)
- L1 Associate Engineer: $85,000 - $105,000
- L2 Engineer: $105,000 - $135,000
- L3 Senior Engineer: $135,000 - $170,000
- L4 Staff Engineer: $170,000 - $210,000
- L5 Principal Engineer: $210,000 - $260,000

## Annual merit review
Merit increases are decided once a year in the March compensation cycle. The merit budget is 4% of total payroll.

## Off-cycle adjustments
Off-cycle raises are limited to 12% of base salary and require approval from the department VP and the Head of People. Counter-offers to retain employees also require VP approval.

## Confidentiality
Individual salary data must never be shared outside the HR team.
