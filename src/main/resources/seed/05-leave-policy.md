# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Paid time off
Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next year; any remaining days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Primary and secondary caregivers are both entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Requesting leave
PTO requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days.
