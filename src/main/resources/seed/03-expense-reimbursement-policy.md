# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting claims
Expense claims must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase. Claims older than 30 days are rejected unless your director approves an exception.

## Receipts
An itemised receipt is required for every expense above $25. Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts.

## Meals and travel
The daily meal allowance during business travel is $75 for domestic trips and $100 for international trips. Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class; business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.

## Payment
Approved expenses are reimbursed through payroll within 10 business days of manager approval.

## Non-reimbursable items
Alcohol outside of approved client entertainment, traffic fines, and personal entertainment are never reimbursed.
