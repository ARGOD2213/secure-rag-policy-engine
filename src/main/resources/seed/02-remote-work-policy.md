# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility and schedule
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams agree on at least one shared in-office anchor day each week.

## Core hours
Regardless of location, employees must be reachable during core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their team's home time zone.

## Equipment and stipend
Each employee receives a one-time home office stipend of $500 to buy a desk, chair or monitor. Company laptops must be used for all work; personal computers are not permitted.

## Security
Remote employees must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems and must not work from public Wi-Fi without the VPN enabled.

## Working from another country
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year with written approval from their manager and the People team. Longer stays require a tax and immigration review.
