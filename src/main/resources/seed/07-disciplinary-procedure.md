# Disciplinary Procedure (HR only)

## Stages
The disciplinary process has three stages: a verbal warning, a written warning, and a final written warning. Gross misconduct may lead to dismissal without prior warnings.

## Investigations
HR must start an investigation within 5 business days of a formal complaint. The employee may bring a colleague or representative to any disciplinary meeting.

## Duration of warnings
A written warning stays on the employee's file for 12 months; a final written warning stays for 18 months.

## Appeals
Employees may appeal any disciplinary decision in writing within 10 business days.
